Ordinary passport applications are processed by the Department of Passports in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu, and by District Administration Offices, Area Administration Offices, and Nepali missions abroad.

Urgent passport service is available at the Department of Passports in Kathmandu. Regular service is available from DAOs, AAOs, and diplomatic missions. e-passport notices and application instructions are published on the official Department of Passports website.

If you are unsure which office covers your district, start with your District Administration Office or the Department of Passports enquiry channels stored in NagrikAI contacts.
