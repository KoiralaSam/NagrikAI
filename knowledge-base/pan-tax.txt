PAN registration, taxpayer services, and most Inland Revenue questions are handled by the Inland Revenue Department or the nearest Inland Revenue Office.

IRD publishes central contact information and office-wise information officer details on its official get-in-touch page. Use that page for current office lists rather than unofficial directories.

If you already have a PAN, take it when you visit a revenue office. If you do not, ask the nearest IRD office how to apply and which identity documents they require.
