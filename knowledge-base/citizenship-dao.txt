Lost citizenship certificates and requests for a copy are handled by your District Administration Office under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

You will need to apply at the DAO that covers your permanent address or the district recorded on your citizenship. Supporting documents commonly include a written application, a copy if any remains, and identity witnesses as required by that office.

Because local requirements vary by district, ask which DAO applies to you before travelling. Use the Ministry of Home Affairs site to find district office information.
